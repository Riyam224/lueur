Privacy Policy

Lueur — Last updated September 9, 2026
Developed by Riyam

Lueur is a wellness and journaling companion app built around Luna, an AI companion who responds to your mood check-ins and journal entries. This page explains exactly what information Lueur collects, why, and how you're in control of it.

Lueur is not a medical device, does not provide therapy or clinical treatment, and is not a substitute for professional mental health care. Luna is a supportive companion for reflection, not a licensed professional.

All of Luna's replies are generated by artificial intelligence (via the third-party model provider described below), not by a human reviewer, and may occasionally be inaccurate or inappropriate. You can flag any response directly in the chat — see "Reporting a Response" below.

Lueur is intended for users aged 18 and over. See "Age Requirement" below.

Our account system technically supports a few optional profile fields (such as a short bio, date of birth, or gender) for possible future personalization features. None of these fields are currently collected, shown, or editable anywhere in the Lueur app — if that ever changes, we'll update this policy and the "last updated" date above before the feature ships.

Content That Stays on Your Device

Saved quotes (Luna's responses you choose to bookmark), drawings you create, and your Sudoku history are all stored locally on your device only — none of this content is ever uploaded to our servers. For breathing exercises, drawings, and Sudoku, we do log that you completed the activity (for your streak and journal timeline), along with basic outcome details — how long a breathing exercise lasted, and for Sudoku whether it was solved, how long it took, and its difficulty — but never the drawing image itself, the puzzle board, or any audio/visual recording of a breathing session.

Guest Mode

You can use Lueur's breathing exercises, drawing canvas, and Sudoku puzzles without creating an account. Content you create in guest mode — such as a drawing — is never sent to Groq and is never stored on our servers; it stays on your device only, and is automatically cleared when the app restarts. Talking with Luna and receiving AI-generated responses requires creating an account.

How Your Data Is Used

  • Your mood entries and journal text are sent securely to our backend server so Luna can generate a thoughtful, personalized response, and so your entries sync across sessions.
  • When Luna generates a response, we send your message and the relevant recent conversation context needed to generate that response to Groq, a third-party AI service — this happens entirely server-to-server; Groq never receives your name, email, or any other identifying account information. Note that the text itself may still contain identifying details if you choose to share them (e.g. mentioning your name).
  • Some data (like recent entries) is also cached locally on your device so the app works smoothly even with a spotty connection.
  • We do not sell your data, do not use your journal content for advertising, and do not show ads in Lueur.
  • We do not use your data for remarketing or third-party ad tracking of any kind.

Conversation Memory

When a chat conversation with Luna comes to a natural close, we generate a short summary of that conversation (also via Groq, subject to the same terms described above) and store it associated with your account. This summary gives Luna continuity — so she can reference something from an earlier conversation without you needing to repeat it. Only the summary is kept; the underlying conversation transcript used to generate it is not separately retained beyond that one-time processing step. This summary is deleted, along with the rest of your data, if you delete your account.

Reporting a Response

If a reply from Luna feels off, you can flag it directly from the chat. This sends the flagged message text, the reason you selected, an optional comment, and — if you choose to include it — your own message that prompted it, to our backend server (hosted on Railway, listed below) so we can review it. This is not sent to Groq. Reported content is tied to your account and is permanently deleted if you delete your account, the same as your journal entries.

If You're Going Through a Hard Moment

If something you share with Luna suggests you may be in crisis, Luna is designed to respond with extra care and to point you toward crisis resources instead of continuing as a normal conversation. This safety response happens automatically and is handled with the same care and data protections described throughout this policy — it does not involve a person reviewing your entry in real time.

Wherever your data is processed, we require these providers to protect it consistently with this policy, and, where applicable, using legally recognized safeguards for cross-border transfer (such as standard contractual clauses).

Advertising & Tracking

Lueur does not show ads and does not use your journal content, mood data, or conversations for advertising or ad personalization of any kind. We do not collect or use the Google Advertising ID (AAID) or any other advertising/cross-app tracking identifier, and we do not participate in any ad network, ad exchange, or third-party remarketing. Firebase Analytics is used solely for aggregate app-usage insight tied to a non-advertising app-instance identifier, as described above.

Delete Your Account & Data

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time, directly in the app:

  1. Open Lueur and go to Profile
  2. Tap Delete Account
  3. Confirm — this is immediate and permanent

Deleting your account permanently removes your account record, your authentication identity, your journal entries, your conversation memory summary, and any content you've reported from our production systems — this cannot be undone through the app. Residual copies may briefly persist in secure backups or service-provider logs, and are removed according to their own retention schedules.

No longer have the app installed? Email us at riyam.thekluge@gmail.com from the address associated with your account, and we'll process the deletion within 30 days.

Deleting Journal Entries Only

If you'd like to clear your journal history without deleting your account entirely, you can delete individual entries, or delete all of your journal entries at once, from within the app. This removes your journal content and Luna's responses but keeps your account, profile, and login active. This does not delete your saved quotes, drawings, or Sudoku history — those are separate, on-device content and aren't affected by clearing your journal.

Your Rights

Wherever you're located — including the EU, UK, or elsewhere with statutory data protection rights — you have the right to access, correct, delete, and receive a portable copy of your personal data:

  • Access & correction — contact us below if you'd like your information corrected or a copy of what we hold; there is currently no self-service profile-editing screen in the app.
  • Deletion — see "Delete Your Account & Data" above.
  • Portability — this is a manual request, not an automated export feature: email us and we'll compile a copy of your data to send you, available anytime you ask, not just before deletion.

US Residents

Regardless of whether a specific US state privacy law technically applies to Lueur, we extend the same practices to all US users as a matter of policy:

  • We do not sell your personal information, and do not "share" it for cross-context behavioral advertising (as those terms are used in laws like California's CCPA/CPRA) — so there is nothing to opt out of.
  • Non-discrimination — we will not deny you service, charge you a different price, or provide a different level of quality because you asked us about your data or requested deletion.
  • If you disagree with how we've handled a request, email us at the address below and we'll review it.

To exercise any of the rights on this page, contact us at riyam.thekluge@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity (typically by confirming the email associated with your account) before fulfilling a request.

Data Retention

We retain your account and journal data for as long as your account remains active. If you delete your account, this data is permanently removed as described above. Crash reports are retained only as long as needed to diagnose and fix issues.

Data Security

We use encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connections between the app and our servers, secure token-based authentication via Firebase, and access controls limiting who can reach production data. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable, concrete steps to protect your information.

Age Requirement & Children's Privacy

Lueur is intended for users who are 18 years of age or older. During sign-up, you're asked to confirm you're 18+ via a self-declaration checkbox (or, for Google Sign-In, a one-time confirmation dialog). This confirmation is stored locally on your device, tied to your account, so you're not asked again — it is never sent to or stored on our servers, and we do not independently verify age through any ID or age-verification service.

Lueur is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18, and in no case from children under 13 (the US COPPA standard). If we become aware that someone under our minimum age has created an account, we will delete the account and associated data. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us at the address below and we'll remove it.
